## Copy-ready prompt

Create one uninterrupted eight-second photorealistic automotive studio take of the exact Pagani Huayra BC in the starting reference. One physical car, one camera, one continuous scene. Preserve the selected livery, body geometry, wheels, stripe pattern and number 20. The number remains attached to its original body panel throughout; it never slides, changes color, duplicates or relocates.

The car stays stationary on the white studio floor. Its wheels do not roll or steer. Only the camera moves during the orbit. Starting at the reference front three-quarter position, the camera follows a smooth circular arc along the flank visible in the starting image, passes through a genuine side-profile view, and arrives at a rear three-quarter view of that SAME flank. Every intermediate viewpoint must be shown through continuous perspective and parallax. Do not cross to the opposite side or reverse orbit direction.

Use the same fixed focal length, camera height and orbit radius for the entire shot, matching the starting image. Keep the optical axis aimed at the vehicle center, the horizon level and the complete car inside the frame with generous room above for raised doors and engine clamshell. Keep exposure, white balance, studio lights and floor reflections consistent. No zoom, lens swap or reframing jump.

Timing within this single take:
- 0.0–0.5 seconds: hold the starting front three-quarter composition; all doors and body panels closed.
- 0.5–4.5 seconds: smoothly orbit along the same flank, through side profile, to the rear three-quarter view. All panels remain closed. Ease the camera to a stop at the end of the arc.
- 4.5–6.0 seconds: KEEP THAT EXACT REAR CAMERA POSITION. Both doors open smoothly on their existing hinges. The car and camera remain stationary.
- 6.0–7.5 seconds: from the SAME CAMERA POSITION, the rear engine clamshell lifts smoothly on its hinges to reveal the engine; doors stay open. Preserve the existing panel and wing assembly relationships.
- 7.5–8.0 seconds: hold the final open-door, exposed-engine composition. No reset to the front.

The motion must read as one filmed physical event. No cuts, dissolves, crossfades, superimposed viewpoints, ghost cars, morphing between photographs, teleporting camera, extra vehicle, duplicated wheels, sliding decals, disappearing panels, turntable rotation, exploded parts, captions or watermark. Deliver a clean 16:9 clip with no audio required.
